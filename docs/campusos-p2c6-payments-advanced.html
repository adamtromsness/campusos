P2-6: Payments Advanced

Financial aid programmes with parent applications and fund pool tracking, fee categories with configurable schedules and grade-level targeting, student lunch accounts with POS-integrated balance management and sibling transfers, payment plans with instalment tracking, automatic invoice generation rules, sibling and early-payment discount rules, credit notes and payment reversals with offsetting ledger entries, late fee automation, and multi-invoice payment allocation. The business office's complete billing toolkit.
May 2026 · 10 Steps · ERD v11 · M84 Payments .1 (17 tables) · FIN-001 + FIN-002 · Phase 2 Cycle 6

Plan Overview

Phase 2 Cycle 6 builds the deferred tables from Cycle 6's M84 Payments & Billing module — 17 new tables across 4 domains: financial aid (3), fee management + auto-invoicing (5), lunch accounts (3), and billing operations (6: payment plans, credit notes, reversals, allocations, discounts, late fees). Cycle 6 built the core billing engine (family accounts, invoices, payments, ledger entries, Stripe integration). This cycle adds the operational depth that a school business office needs for day-to-day billing: "How do we handle siblings who get 10% off? What happens when a family can't pay the full amount at once? How do we charge lunch automatically?"

Every financial table in this cycle follows the M84 event contract: mutations that affect the ledger emit Kafka events (pay.credit_note.issued, pay.refund.issued, pay.debt.written_off) that the Cycle 26 GLConsumer auto-posts as balanced journal entries. The lunch account POS integration connects to Cycle 20's food service module — when a student scans their ID at the cafeteria, fds.meal.served debits the lunch account.

Financial Integrity — IMMUTABLE Tables

pay_credit_notes, pay_payment_reversals, and pay_lunch_account_balance_transfers are all IMMUTABLE — no UPDATE, no DELETE at the service layer. Financial corrections are made by creating new offsetting entries, never by modifying existing ones. This is the same pattern as fin_gl_entries from Cycle 26 and ensures the ledger is an append-only audit trail.

Vertical Slice Deliverable

Admin creates fee categories (Tuition, Activity Fee, Exam Fee) → creates fee schedules: Tuition $10K/year ANNUAL all grades, Activity Fee $500/term TERM all grades, GCSE Exam Fee $200 ONE_TIME grade_level="10" → configures auto-invoice rule: TERM_START trigger, 14 days before term, generates Tuition + Activity invoices → configures sibling discount: 2nd child 10%, 3rd child 20% → AutoInvoiceWorker fires at term start: 200 invoices generated in batch, sibling discounts auto-applied, generation run tracked → parent David Chen receives $10,000 tuition invoice → requests payment plan: 10 monthly instalments of $1,000 → plan ACTIVE, first instalment due date set → David also applies for financial aid (BAND_C household income, uploads tax return) → finance admin reviews, approves 15% reduction ($1,500 award from "Need-Based Aid" programme, fund_remaining decremented) → award applied to next invoice as credit → separately, Maya's lunch account loaded with $50 deposit → Maya scans ID at cafeteria 3 times this week ($4.50 each) → lunch balance = $36.50 → balance hits low_balance_threshold ($10) → parent notified → end of year: Maya's remaining $6.50 transferred to sibling Ethan's lunch account → meanwhile, a parent's payment bounces → pay_payment_reversals created (BOUNCED_CHEQUE, IMMUTABLE) → invoice reinstated to OVERDUE → 7-day grace period passes → LateFeesWorker adds $25 late fee line item → admin issues credit note for the late fee as a goodwill gesture → pay_credit_notes created (IMMUTABLE) with offsetting ledger entry.

What's In / What's Deferred

In scope (17 tables): pay_financial_aid_programs, pay_financial_aid_awards, pay_financial_aid_applications, pay_fee_categories, pay_fee_schedules, pay_auto_invoice_rules, pay_invoice_generation_runs, pay_discount_rules, pay_lunch_accounts, pay_lunch_transactions, pay_lunch_account_balance_transfers, pay_payment_plans, pay_credit_notes, pay_payment_reversals, pay_payment_allocations, pay_late_payment_policies, pay_saved_payment_methods. Functions FIN-001 extended + FIN-002.

Deferred: Stripe ACH/direct debit for auto-pay instalment collection. Saved payment method tokenisation (schema ready; Stripe SetupIntent integration deferred). Financial aid means-testing calculator. Lunch account auto-replenishment via Stripe. Sibling discount matrix UI builder. Multi-currency support. Tax receipt generation for donations. NSLP free/reduced meal eligibility integration.

Step-by-Step Build Plan

01Financial Aid Schema (3 tables)
Schema
  1. pay_financial_aid_programs — school_id UUID, name TEXT NOT NULL, reduction_type TEXT NOT NULL 2-value CHECK (PERCENTAGE, FIXED_AMOUNT), reduction_value NUMERIC(8,2), total_fund_amount NUMERIC(12,2) nullable (null=uncapped), fund_remaining NUMERIC(12,2) nullable (materialised: total - SUM active awards). UNIQUE(school_id, name).
  2. pay_financial_aid_awards — student_id UUID FK, program_id UUID FK, academic_year_id UUID FK, award_amount NUMERIC(10,2), approved_by UUID FK, effective_from DATE, effective_to DATE. UNIQUE(student_id, program_id, academic_year_id). On INSERT: program.fund_remaining decremented.
  3. pay_financial_aid_applications — school_id UUID, student_id UUID FK, program_id UUID FK, guardian_id UUID FK, academic_year_id UUID FK, household_income_band TEXT 5-value CHECK (BAND_A..BAND_E), supporting_documents JSONB, application_statement TEXT, status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'DRAFT' 6-value CHECK (DRAFT, SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED, WITHDRAWN), reviewed_by UUID FK, reviewer_notes TEXT, award_id UUID FK nullable. INDEX(school_id, academic_year_id, status).
Produces → 3 tables. Migration: 108_pay_financial_aid.sql.
02Fee Management + Auto-Invoicing Schema (5 tables)
Schema
  1. pay_fee_categories — school_id UUID, name TEXT NOT NULL, is_mandatory BOOLEAN. UNIQUE(school_id, name).
  2. pay_fee_schedules — school_id UUID, fee_category_id UUID FK, academic_year_id UUID FK, name TEXT, amount NUMERIC(10,2), due_date DATE, frequency TEXT 4-value CHECK (ONE_TIME, MONTHLY, TERM, ANNUAL), grade_level TEXT nullable, applies_to_student_ids UUID[] nullable.
  3. pay_auto_invoice_rules — school_id UUID, name TEXT NOT NULL, trigger_type TEXT NOT NULL 4-value CHECK (ENROLMENT_CONFIRMED, TERM_START, DATE_OF_MONTH, ACADEMIC_YEAR_START), fee_schedule_id UUID FK, trigger_day_of_month INT, trigger_term_offset_days INT, applies_to_grade_level TEXT, is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT true. UNIQUE(school_id, name).
  4. pay_invoice_generation_runs — school_id UUID, run_type TEXT 3-value CHECK (MANUAL_BATCH, AUTO_RULE_TRIGGERED, FEE_SCHEDULE_BULK), fee_schedule_id UUID FK, auto_rule_id UUID FK, academic_year_id UUID FK, initiated_by UUID FK, total_families_targeted INT, invoices_created INT, invoices_skipped INT, invoices_failed INT, status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'QUEUED' 4-value CHECK (QUEUED, RUNNING, COMPLETED, FAILED), started_at TIMESTAMPTZ, completed_at TIMESTAMPTZ. INDEX(school_id, status).
  5. pay_discount_rules — school_id UUID, name TEXT NOT NULL, discount_type TEXT 6-value CHECK (SIBLING, EARLY_PAYMENT, LOYALTY, BURSARY, STAFF_CHILD, CUSTOM), calculation_method TEXT 2-value CHECK (PERCENTAGE, FIXED_AMOUNT), value NUMERIC(8,2), applies_to_fee_category_id UUID FK, sibling_order INT nullable, minimum_invoice_amount NUMERIC(8,2), academic_year_id UUID FK, is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT true. UNIQUE(school_id, name).
Produces → 5 tables. Migration: 109_pay_fees_invoicing.sql.
03Lunch Accounts Schema (3 tables)
Schema

NO UPDATE, NO DELETE.

04Billing Operations Schema (6 tables)
Schema
  1. pay_payment_plans — invoice_id UUID FK, total_instalments INT, installment_amount NUMERIC(10,2), next_due_date DATE, status TEXT 3-value CHECK (ACTIVE, COMPLETED, DEFAULTED).
  2. pay_credit_notes — IMMUTABLE. school_id UUID, invoice_id UUID FK, line_item_id UUID FK nullable, credit_amount NUMERIC(10,2), reason TEXT NOT NULL, issued_by UUID FK, issued_at TIMESTAMPTZ. On INSERT: pay_ledger_entries CREDIT row. Emits pay.credit_note.issued. NO UPDATE, NO DELETE.
  3. pay_payment_reversals — IMMUTABLE. school_id UUID, payment_id UUID FK UNIQUE, reversal_type TEXT 5-value CHECK (BOUNCED_CHEQUE, RECALLED_TRANSFER, CHARGEBACK, DUPLICATE_PAYMENT, OTHER), reversal_reason TEXT NOT NULL, bank_reference TEXT, reversed_by UUID FK, reversed_at TIMESTAMPTZ. On INSERT: payment.status=FAILED, invoice reinstated. NO UPDATE, NO DELETE.
  4. pay_payment_allocations — payment_id UUID FK, invoice_id UUID FK, allocated_amount NUMERIC(10,2). UNIQUE(payment_id, invoice_id). SUM(allocated) must equal payment.amount.
  5. pay_late_payment_policies — school_id UUID UNIQUE, is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT false, grace_period_days INT DEFAULT 7, fee_type TEXT 2-value CHECK (FIXED, PERCENTAGE_MONTHLY), fee_amount NUMERIC(8,2), fee_percentage NUMERIC(5,4), max_late_fee_amount NUMERIC(8,2), applies_to_fee_category_id UUID FK. LateFeesWorker runs nightly.
  6. pay_saved_payment_methods — family_account_id UUID FK, stripe_payment_method_id TEXT NOT NULL, card_last_four TEXT, card_brand TEXT, is_default BOOLEAN DEFAULT false, added_at TIMESTAMPTZ. INDEX(family_account_id). Token-only — no card numbers stored.
Produces → 6 tables. Migration: 111_pay_billing_ops.sql. Total P2-6 tables: 17.

06Financial Aid + Fee Management NestJS Module
Code
  1. FinancialAidService — GET /payments/financial-aid/programs, POST /payments/financial-aid/programs. POST /payments/financial-aid/applications (parent submits with documents), GET /payments/financial-aid/applications (admin: all; parent: own), PATCH /payments/financial-aid/applications/:id (approve → creates award, decrements fund_remaining). GET /payments/financial-aid/awards/:studentId.
  2. FeeScheduleService — CRUD for fee categories + fee schedules. POST /payments/fee-schedules/:id/generate-invoices (manual batch generation via pay_invoice_generation_runs).
  3. AutoInvoiceService — CRUD for auto-invoice rules. AutoInvoiceWorker: polls rules by trigger_type, generates invoices via pay_invoice_generation_runs, applies discount rules at generation time (sibling detection from sis_student_guardians), tracks created/skipped/failed.
  4. DiscountRuleService — CRUD for discount rules. Applied automatically during invoice generation. Sibling order computed from sis_student_guardians + enrolment dates.
Produces → 4 services + 1 worker, ~16 endpoints.
07Lunch Accounts + Billing Operations NestJS Module
Code
  1. LunchAccountService — GET /payments/lunch-accounts/:studentId (balance + recent transactions), POST /payments/lunch-accounts/:id/deposit (parent adds funds), POST /payments/lunch-accounts/:id/charge (POS debit on fds.meal.served consumer), POST /payments/lunch-accounts/transfer (sibling transfer or year-end rollover, IMMUTABLE). GET /payments/lunch-accounts/low-balance (admin: students below threshold). Emits pay.lunch.low_balance for parent notification.
  2. PaymentPlanService — POST /payments/invoices/:id/payment-plan (create plan with instalment schedule), GET /payments/payment-plans, PATCH /payments/payment-plans/:id (record instalment payment, advance next_due_date). InstalmentsWorker: nightly check for overdue instalments → mark DEFAULTED after configurable threshold.
  3. CreditNoteService — POST /payments/invoices/:id/credit-note (IMMUTABLE: creates credit + offsetting ledger entry; emits pay.credit_note.issued). GET /payments/credit-notes.
  4. ReversalService — POST /payments/payments/:id/reverse (IMMUTABLE: payment.status=FAILED, invoice reinstated; emits pay.payment.reversed). GET /payments/reversals.
  5. PaymentAllocationService — POST /payments/payments/:id/allocate (split payment across multiple invoices; validates SUM = payment.amount).
  6. LateFeeService — CRUD for late payment policy. LateFeesWorker: nightly scan for invoices past due_date + grace_period → creates pay_invoice_line_items with late fee amount (capped at max).
Produces → 6 services + 2 workers, ~18 endpoints, 3 Kafka emits. Total P2-6 endpoints: ~34.

10Kafka Consumer: fds.meal.served → Lunch Account Debit
Code
  1. LunchAccountConsumer — Subscribes to dev.fds.meal.served from Cycle 20 Food Service. On event: looks up pay_lunch_accounts for the student_id, creates pay_lunch_transactions (MEAL_CHARGE), decrements balance. If balance ≤ low_balance_threshold: emits pay.lunch.low_balance. Idempotent via transaction dedup on (lunch_account_id, meal_date, pos_device_id, served_at).
Produces → 1 new Kafka consumer (LunchAccountConsumer on fds.meal.served). Lunch account auto-debited on cafeteria POS scan.
