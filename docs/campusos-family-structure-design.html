CampusOS|Design Hub|FAMILY STRUCTURE DESIGN

Family Structure & Person Relationships

Representing biological, legal, and step-family relationships between people — separate from the household/account management model. Enables custody-aware school operations, derived sibling detection, and family tree visualization.

Two Separate Concepts

Household (existing): who lives together, manages accounts, picks up children. Modeled by platform_families + platform_family_members + platform_family_children. A child of divorced parents belongs to two households.

Family Structure (new): who is biologically or legally related to whom. Modeled by platform_person_relationships. A child has exactly one set of biological parents regardless of how many households they belong to. Step-parents, half-siblings, and custody arrangements live here.

DECISION — Auto-reciprocal relationships

When a user creates a relationship "Alivia → BIOLOGICAL_MOTHER → Ashley", the system automatically creates the reciprocal "Ashley → BIOLOGICAL_CHILD → Alivia". This ensures the family tree is navigable from either direction. Deleting one side deletes both.

2. Schema

platform_person_relationships (new table)

CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS platform.platform_person_relationships (
  id                  UUID PRIMARY KEY,
  person_id           UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES platform.iam_person(id),
  related_person_id   UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES platform.iam_person(id),
  relationship_type   TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (relationship_type IN (
    'BIOLOGICAL_MOTHER', 'BIOLOGICAL_FATHER', 'BIOLOGICAL_CHILD',
    'ADOPTIVE_MOTHER', 'ADOPTIVE_FATHER', 'ADOPTIVE_CHILD',
    'STEP_MOTHER', 'STEP_FATHER', 'STEP_CHILD',
    'LEGAL_GUARDIAN', 'LEGAL_WARD',
    'SPOUSE', 'DOMESTIC_PARTNER',
    'GRANDPARENT', 'GRANDCHILD'
  )),
  is_legal_custody    BOOLEAN DEFAULT false,
  custody_arrangement TEXT,         -- 'FULL', 'JOINT', 'WEEKDAYS', 'WEEKENDS', 'SUMMERS', 'SUPERVISED', 'NONE'
  custody_notes       TEXT,         -- free text: "Every other weekend", "School year only"
  is_primary_residence BOOLEAN DEFAULT false,  -- does the child primarily live with this person?
  verified            BOOLEAN DEFAULT false,   -- school has verified documentation
  verified_by         UUID REFERENCES platform.iam_person(id),
  verified_at         TIMESTAMPTZ,
  start_date          DATE,         -- when the relationship started (adoption date, marriage date)
  end_date            DATE,         -- when it ended (divorce date) — NULL = current
  created_by          UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES platform.iam_person(id),
  created_at          TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
  updated_at          TIMESTAMPTZ,
  UNIQUE (person_id, related_person_id, relationship_type),
  CHECK (person_id != related_person_id)
);

CREATE INDEX idx_person_relationships_person ON platform.platform_person_relationships(person_id);
CREATE INDEX idx_person_relationships_related ON platform.platform_person_relationships(related_person_id);

3. Derived Relationships

The system computes these automatically from the stored relationships — they are never stored directly.

Sibling detection

Derived TypeLogicExample
Full siblingShare BOTH biological mother AND biological fatherAlivia and Thatcher have the same mother (Ashley) and father (Adam)
Half-sibling (maternal)Share biological mother but different biological fatherAlivia (mother: Ashley, father: Adam) and Jake (mother: Ashley, father: Carlos)
Half-sibling (paternal)Share biological father but different biological motherAlivia (father: Adam, mother: Ashley) and Emma (father: Adam, mother: Sarah)
Step-siblingNo shared biological parents, but a parent's spouse/partner is the other child's biological parentAlivia (parents: Adam + Ashley) and Jake (parents: Sarah + Carlos), where Adam married Sarah
Adoptive siblingShare an adoptive parentTwo children both adopted by Adam and Ashley

Derivation query (siblings)

-- Find all siblings of a given person
WITH person_parents AS (
  -- Get this person's biological/adoptive parents
  SELECT related_person_id AS parent_id, relationship_type
  FROM platform.platform_person_relationships
  WHERE person_id = $1
    AND relationship_type IN ('BIOLOGICAL_MOTHER','BIOLOGICAL_FATHER',
                               'ADOPTIVE_MOTHER','ADOPTIVE_FATHER')
    AND end_date IS NULL
),
sibling_candidates AS (
  -- Find other children of those parents
  SELECT pr.person_id AS sibling_id,
         pr.related_person_id AS shared_parent_id,
         pr.relationship_type
  FROM platform.platform_person_relationships pr
  JOIN person_parents pp ON pr.related_person_id = pp.parent_id
  WHERE pr.relationship_type IN ('BIOLOGICAL_CHILD','ADOPTIVE_CHILD')
    AND pr.person_id != $1
    AND pr.end_date IS NULL
)
SELECT sibling_id,
       COUNT(DISTINCT shared_parent_id) AS shared_parent_count,
       CASE
         WHEN COUNT(DISTINCT shared_parent_id) = 2 THEN 'FULL_SIBLING'
         WHEN COUNT(DISTINCT shared_parent_id) = 1 THEN 'HALF_SIBLING'
       END AS sibling_type
FROM sibling_candidates
GROUP BY sibling_id;

4. Custody Model

Why custody matters for schools

Schools need to know: who can pick up this child? Who receives report cards? Who can authorize a field trip? Who should NOT be given access? Custody is not binary — it's a spectrum: full custody, joint custody, weekday/weekend splits, supervised-only, no contact. The custody_arrangement and custody_notes fields capture this nuance.

Custody ArrangementMeaningSchool impact
FULLThis parent has sole legal and physical custodyPrimary contact for everything. Other parent may have restricted or no access.
JOINTBoth parents share legal and physical custodyBoth parents receive communications. Both can authorize. Both can pick up.
WEEKDAYSThis parent has custody on school daysPrimary contact during the school week. Emergency contact Mon-Fri.
WEEKENDSThis parent has custody on weekends/holidaysContact for weekend events. May need separate mailing address.
SUMMERSThis parent has custody during summer breakSummer program contact. Address may change seasonally.
SUPERVISEDThis parent has supervised visitation onlyCannot pick up alone. Cannot authorize without other parent's consent.
NONENo custody — may have visitation rightsNot authorized for pickup. Not a decision-maker. May still be listed as biological parent for records.

DECISION — Custody does NOT control CampusOS household access

Custody arrangement is informational for schools. It does NOT automatically restrict a parent's access within CampusOS. If a parent is a guardian in a household, they have full access to that household's data. Custody-based access restrictions (e.g., "Father cannot see grades") are enforced at the school level through sis_student_guardians.access_level, not through the family structure. The family structure provides the information; the school decides what to do with it.

If the mother is a CampusOS user (selected from search): related_person_id is set. The auto-reciprocal is created. Their profile will also show this relationship.

If entered manually (not on CampusOS): create a minimal iam_person record as a placeholder (first_name, last_name only, no account). This allows the family tree to show the name even if the person never joins CampusOS. Alternatively, store the name directly on the relationship row — but this breaks if they later join and should be linked.

DECISION — Non-CampusOS parents

For biological parents who are not on CampusOS (e.g., deceased parent, absent parent, parent who will never create an account): store the name directly on the relationship row using optional related_person_name field. Do NOT create a placeholder iam_person for someone who will never use the system. If they later join, the relationship can be updated to link to their iam_person.

Schema addition for non-CampusOS people

-- Add to platform_person_relationships:
  related_person_id     UUID REFERENCES platform.iam_person(id),  -- NOW NULLABLE
  related_person_name   TEXT,  -- used when related_person_id is NULL
  -- CHECK: at least one of related_person_id or related_person_name must be set
  CHECK (related_person_id IS NOT NULL OR related_person_name IS NOT NULL)

Family structure data is highly sensitive. Custody arrangements, divorce status, and absent parent information must be carefully access-controlled.

10. Scenarios

Scenario A — Simple family (no divorce)

  1. Adam registers, adds Ashley as guardian, adds 4 children.
  2. On Scout's profile, Adam sets: biological mother = Ashley, biological father = Adam.
  3. System auto-creates reciprocals on Ashley's and Adam's profiles.
  4. System derives: Thatcher, Sailor, Harper are full siblings of Scout (same parents).
  5. One household. One family tree. Simple.

Scenario B — Divorced parents, two households

  1. Adam and Ashley divorce. Adam marries Sarah.
  2. Adam creates a new household: "The Tromsness-Johnson Family" with Sarah.
  3. Scout belongs to BOTH households (Adam's and Ashley's).
  4. On Scout's profile: biological mother = Ashley (joint custody, even weeks), biological father = Adam (joint custody, odd weeks), step-mother = Sarah.
  5. Sarah has a son Jake from a previous relationship. Jake's biological father = Carlos.
  6. System derives: Scout and Jake are step-siblings (no shared parents, but parent's spouse is Jake's parent).
  7. School admin sees both households, custody schedule, and the blended family structure.

Scenario C — Foster child

  1. School enrolls a foster child, Marcus.
  2. Foster parents (the Johnsons) are set as LEGAL_GUARDIAN with custody = FULL.
  3. Biological parents may be entered by name only (no CampusOS account): "Maria Garcia" (biological mother), father unknown.
  4. Marcus belongs to the Johnson household.
  5. If Marcus moves to a new foster family, the old relationship gets end_date = today, new LEGAL_GUARDIAN relationship is created.

Scenario D — Grandparent as guardian

  1. Debbie Tromsness (grandmother) has legal guardianship of her grandchild Emma.
  2. On Emma's profile: GRANDPARENT = Debbie (is_legal_custody = true, custody = FULL).
  3. Biological parents may be listed but with custody = NONE.
  4. Debbie is in the household as a guardian. She manages Emma's account.
